Where Ideas Become Reality

September 22, 2025 | 2:00 PM - 4:00 PM | C-210

Event Overview

Ready to create something impactful?

Use the power of the Internet of Things (IoT) to solve real-life problems! This is your chance to turn innovative ideas into working prototypes that can make a difference in people's lives.

Event Details

  • Date: September 22, 2025
  • Venue: To be announced
  • Format: Individual/Team
  • Entry: Free registration

What to Do

  • Build an IoT solution
  • Use available components
  • Demo your project
  • Network with makers

Project Categories

Smart Home Automation

Control lights, fans, appliances remotely. Create intelligent home systems that respond to user needs and environmental conditions.

Safety & Alert Systems

Build intruder detection, fire alerts, emergency notification systems. Focus on keeping people and property safe.

Smart Detectors

Create water level sensors, gas leak detectors, air quality monitors. Monitor environmental conditions automatically.

Health Monitoring

Develop elderly care systems, patient monitoring devices, medication reminders. Technology for better healthcare.

Smart Irrigation

Build automated water-saving solutions, soil moisture monitoring, weather-responsive watering systems.

Open Innovation

Have another idea? Build anything that solves a real-world problem using IoT technology!

Available Components

What You Can Use: We'll provide access to various IoT components and development boards. You can also bring your own components if you have specific requirements.

Microcontrollers

Arduino, ESP32, Raspberry Pi

Sensors

Temperature, Humidity, Motion, Light

Actuators

Motors, Servos, Relays, Pumps

Displays

LCD, OLED, LED matrices

Connectivity

WiFi, Bluetooth modules

Power

Batteries, Power supplies

Project Guidelines

  • Focus on solving real-world problems
  • Use IoT technology meaningfully
  • Create working prototypes
  • Prepare a brief demo/presentation
  • Document your solution
  • Be ready to explain the impact

Remember: Build it - Bring it - Let's see what you create!

This is a hands-on event where you'll work with real hardware to create tangible solutions. Come prepared to get your hands dirty with circuits, code, and creativity!
